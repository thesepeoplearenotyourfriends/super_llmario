LLMario ASCII interchange

ASCII is an editor clipboard sketch. Import compiles symbols into native map objects.

X terrain · # block · o coin · * star · e enemy · ? sign · O portal · @ player · P pipe mouth · p pipe body
v2 assigns pipe aliases A…Z, AA… by mouth X then Y. @pipe A -> B stores topology; @pipe-prop A solid=false scale=1.5 preserves non-default pipe semantics. The visible grid remains authoritative.
